Apply the supplied codebook. Write complete propositions, preserving negation, modality,
quantifiers, actor privileges, scope and conditions in the proposition itself.

infer missing protections, authentication requirements, data flows or impact

Do not force all families or any fixed number of claims. Omit a separate title

list if there are no extractable assertions.

Decompose propositions before assigning families. Sentence boundaries are not
claim boundaries: if one sentence contains multiple independently assessable
assertions, return a separate claim for each. Do not summarize each sentence as
one claim assigned to its dominant family. Distinguish asserted operation/location,

passages may support several different claims, including different families.
Do not duplicate the same proposition just to assign more families. Keep enough
subject and context for each proposition to be understandable. Preserve conditions,
negation, modality, alternatives and causal links during splitting; do not assert
that a condition actually holds merely because the report mentions it.
Check that every independently assessable assertion is covered, including multiple
assertions within a sentence. A claim from a later sentence may also cite an earlier
sentence to resolve references. Do not aim for a predetermined claim count.

Return only a JSON object matching the supplied schema. Use local claim IDs
C01, C02, etc.; suffixes such as C09a are allowed. For each source quote, provide
the exact text from title or report, including whitespace, and its one-based
occurrence within that field. Occurrences are counted by starting position,
including overlapping matches. Do not calculate character offsets yourself.
